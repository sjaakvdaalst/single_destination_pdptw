\section{Literature List}


\paragraph{Dumas, Desrosiers, and Soumis (1991) -- The Pickup and Delivery Problem with Time Windows}


Dumas et al.~\cite{dumasPickupDeliveryProblem1991} study the pickup and
delivery problem with time windows (PDPTW). Unlike the PDPTW variant we study,
the standard PDPTW allows vehicles to serve requests with arbitrary pickup and delivery locations,
rather than restricting all requests currently on-board to share a common physical destination.

The paper's main contribution is the first exact solution algorithm for
the PDPTW: a set-partitioning formulation whose LP relaxation is solved
by column generation, with columns priced by a constrained shortest-path
subproblem and a branch-and-bound tree used to resolve fractional
solutions by branching on pickup sequences.


\paragraph{Savelsbergh and Sol (1995) -- The General Pickup and Delivery Problem}


Savelsbergh and Sol~\cite{savelsberghGeneralPickupDelivery1995} study the
general pickup and delivery problem (PDP). The difference to the standard PDPTW
is that the PDP does not include time windows (and unlike 1D-PDPTW, no single-destination requirement).

Their main contribution is definitional and survey-oriented rather than algorithmic:
they formalize the PDPTW problem definition and catalogue the variety of objective functions used
in the literature (e.g., minimizing fleet size, total travel cost, total
duration, or combinations thereof), which we draw on directly when
positioning our own cost-minimization objective.


\paragraph{Diana and Dessouky (2004) -- A New Regret Insertion Heuristic for Solving Large-Scale Dial-a-Ride Problems with Time Windows}


Diana and Dessouky~\cite{dianaNewRegretInsertion2004} study a large-scale
dial-a-ride problem with time windows (DARPTW). This is a passenger-transport
variant of the PDPTW that adds ride-time constraints our 1D-PDPTW does not impose.

Their main contribution is a parallel regret insertion heuristic for the
DARP. A regret-based insertion heuristic is a construction heuristic that
iteratively inserts requests into a partial solution in order of their regret value,
which is the difference in cost between the best and second-best feasible insertion positions
for that request.


\paragraph{Cordeau (2006) -- A Branch-and-Cut Algorithm for the Dial-a-Ride Problem}


Cordeau~\cite{cordeauBranchandCutAlgorithmDialaRide2006} studies the
dial-a-ride problem and develops a branch-and-cut algorithm.



\paragraph{Ropke, Cordeau, and Laporte (2007) -- Models and Branch-and-Cut Algorithms for Pickup and Delivery Problems with Time Windows}


Ropke et al.~\cite{ropkeModelsBranchandcutAlgorithms2007} study the
pickup and delivery problem with time windows and the closely related
dial-a-ride problem.


\paragraph{Ropke and Cordeau (2009) -- Branch and Cut and Price for the Pickup and Delivery Problem with Time Windows}


Ropke and Cordeau~\cite{ropkeBranchCutPrice2009} study the pickup and
delivery problem with time windows.


\paragraph{Ghilas, Demir, and Van Woensel (2016) -- The Pickup and Delivery Problem with Time Windows and Scheduled Lines}


Ghilas et al.~\cite{ghilasPickupDeliveryProblem2016} study a variant of
the pickup and delivery problem with time windows in which scheduled
public transportation services can be used.


\paragraph{Gschwind, Irnich, Rothenbächer, and Tilk (2018) -- Bidirectional Labeling in Column-Generation Algorithms for Pickup-and-Delivery Problems}


Gschwind et al.~\cite{gschwindBidirectionalLabelingColumngeneration2018}
study acceleration techniques for column-generation algorithms for
pickup-and-delivery problems.


\paragraph{Potvin and Rousseau (1993) -- A Parallel Route Building Algorithm for the Vehicle Routing and Scheduling Problem with Time Windows}


Potvin and Rousseau~\cite{potvinParallelRouteBuilding1993} study the
vehicle routing and scheduling problem with time windows.


\paragraph{Shaw (1998) -- Using Constraint Programming and Local Search Methods to Solve Vehicle Routing Problems}


Shaw~\cite{shawUsingConstraintProgramming1998} studies the application of
constraint programming and large-neighborhood search methods to vehicle
routing problems.
